Markó Café

A dialogue between urban rhythm and intimate silence within the Sophia Hotel heritage site.

Client Bursa Hotel Group
Year 2023
Area 333 m²

The Challenge

To transform a heritage site into a modern day-to-night venue without losing the historical soul of the architecture.

Markó is not just a functional addition to the hotel. It is an independent organism that changes its mood throughout the day. The space needed to transition seamlessly from a bright, airy breakfast venue into a moody, intimate evening cocktail bar.

Our approach was to use materials that react to light: polished brass, deep walnut wood, and textured plaster. The interplay of shadow and reflection creates a dynamic atmosphere that requires minimal artificial intervention.

Heritage Context

The name references Marko Vovchok, symbolizing progressive values. The interior reflects this through restoration of original brickwork dating back to 1902 and the seamless integration of modern HVAC systems without visual intrusion.

Custom lighting designed in collaboration with Expolight allows the mood to shift dramatically from morning coffee service to late-night cocktails, effectively doubling the venue's utility.
